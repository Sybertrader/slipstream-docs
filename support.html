Slipstream Support

Customers get help with Slipstream for Jira Cloud by email. Critical Path Works Ltd answers product questions, install issues, and release-radar questions from the address below.

Contact and response

Email support@criticalpathworks.com. Critical Path Works Ltd replies within 24 business hours.

Data and privacy

Critical Path Works Ltd never asks customers to export a database or share raw Jira data. Slipstream runs entirely on Atlassian Forge infrastructure.

What to include in your request

  • Your Jira Cloud site URL (*.atlassian.net)
  • Affected project key(s) or Fix Version name
  • A brief description of the issue or question, plus screenshots if relevant
